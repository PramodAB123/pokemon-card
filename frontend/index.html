GitStar Explorer: turn your GitHub profile into a trading card

Enter any GitHub username and get a free, shareable trading card built from public GitHub stats. Your commits set your level and fleet rank, your top language picks your star system, and your repositories, stars and account age power your shield.

How it works

  1. Type a GitHub username. No sign-up or GitHub login needed.
  2. GitStar Explorer reads public data: commits, repositories, stars and languages.
  3. Your card is ready in seconds, with its own link to share on LinkedIn, X or Reddit.

Frequently asked questions

Is GitStar Explorer free?

Yes. It is free and needs no sign-up.

What GitHub data does it use?

Only public GitHub data: your profile, public repositories, stars, languages and commit activity. No sign-in or access token is needed.

How is my level calculated?

Your level grows with your total commits on a curve from 1 to 99, and your level sets your fleet rank: Cadet, Lieutenant, Commander, Admiral or Fleet Admiral.

Can I share my card?

Yes. Every card has its own link, and the share buttons copy a ready-made caption and open LinkedIn, X or Reddit.
