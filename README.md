## 1. Overview

**GitStar Explorer** turns your open-source contributions into a collectible interstellar pilot profile:
* Your **commits, PRs, and stars** become **Mission Points (MP)** and **Shield Energy**.
* Your **top programming language** assigns your **Star System** and visual theme.
* Your **activity level** unlocks **Fleet Ranks** (Cadet to Fleet Admiral) and **Clearance Borders** (Standard to Black Ops).

---

## 2. How to Generate Your Card

1. **Visit the Live Application**:  
   Open [https://pokitrainer.vercel.app](https://pokitrainer.vercel.app) in your web browser.
2. **Enter Your Callsign**:  
   Type any valid GitHub username (e.g., `torvalds`, `sindresorhus`, `gaearon`, or your own username). Leading `@` symbols and extra whitespace are automatically sanitized.
3. **Execute Warp Jump**:  
   Click **WARP JUMP →** or press `Enter`.
4. **Quick Scout Chips**:  
   Click on any of the pre-loaded **Scout** chips or recent flight history badges to jump straight to that explorer's card.

### Card Sections Explained:
* **Header & Shield**: Displays pilot callsign, avatar lens, and computed **Shield Energy** (ranging from 50 to 999).
* **Dossier Panel**: Displays your **Fleet Rank**, **Clearance Tier**, and unique 4-digit **Vessel ID**.
* **Star System Banner**: Identifies your primary programming ecosystem and overall **Level** (`LVL 1` to `LVL 99`).
* **Mission Capabilities**:
  * ⚡ **Warp Commit**: Total recorded commits
  * 🛸 **Orbital Deploy**: Total public repositories
  * 📡 **Signal Broadcast**: Total programming languages mastered
  * 🔭 **Deep Scan**: Total days since your GitHub account commissioning
* **Reactor Core (MP)**: Visual plasma gauge tracking your Mission Points progress toward the next Level threshold.
* **Subspace Telemetry Footer**: Shows counter-system **Vulnerability**, Vessel ID, and GitHub creation year.

## 5. Fleet Ranks & Clearance Tiers

### Fleet Ranks (Based on Level)
* ━ **Cadet**: Level 1 – 10
* ━━ **Lieutenant**: Level 11 – 25
* ━━━ **Commander**: Level 26 – 50
* ★ **Admiral**: Level 51 – 80
* ★★ **Fleet Admiral**: Level 81 – 99

### Clearance Rarity Borders
* ● **STANDARD**: Clean basic fleet issue border.
* ◆ **CLASSIFIED**: Silver metallic border with ambient glow.
* ★ **TOP SECRET**: Gold metallic border with bright solar aura.
* ✦ **ULTRA**: Purple obsidian border with holographic foil sheen.
* ✦ **BLACK OPS**: Cyan/pink neon pulsing border reserved for elite contributors.

## 6. Interactive Card Controls & Sharing

### 3D Parallax Tilt Effect
When viewing your card on desktop, move your mouse over the trading card to trigger **3D interactive parallax tilt physics** with dynamic holographic light reflections!

### Sharing Your Card
Every explorer card has a direct shareable URL:
```text
https://pokitrainer.vercel.app/YOUR_GITHUB_USERNAME
```
* Click **Transmit Coordinates ✦** at the bottom of your card to copy your unique subspace link directly to your clipboard.
* Share your link on social media, developer forums, or your GitHub profile README.

## 7. 404 Radar & Uncharted Anomalies

If you enter a callsign that does not exist in the galactic GitHub registry:
* You are safely directed to the **404 Deep Space Radar** page.
* An active sweeping radar HUD will display `SECTOR: 0x404 // UNCHARTED_VOID`.
* Use the **Recovery Beacons** (`@torvalds`, `@sindresorhus`, etc.) or click **← RETURN TO COMMAND BRIDGE** to resume exploration.
